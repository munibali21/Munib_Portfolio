POWER BI / CASE STUDY

Jeeny Sales Analysis

A mobility sales analytics dashboard designed to analyze ride-hailing business performance across customers, products and sales activity. The report provides interactive insights into sales trends and customer behavior to support data-driven business decisions.

Power BI DAX Sales Analytics Interactive Reporting
01 / OVERVIEW

Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Jeeny's ride-hailing business data through interactive views of overall performance, customer activity and product/service performance. The dashboard provides a structured way to explore business trends, customer behavior and performance metrics, helping identify key patterns and supporting data-driven decisions within the mobility business.

02 / KEY FEATURES

What the report delivers

  • Tracks conversion, acceptance and cancellation rates
  • Identifies the hours with the highest number of rides boarded
  • Analyzes weekly trends for requested, accepted, cancelled and offered rides
  • Highlights hours with the highest number of available drivers
  • Identifies peak hours based on average ride requests
  • >Provides interactive insights into ride demand and operational performance
